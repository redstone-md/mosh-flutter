# Onboarding illustrations

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-10-02. The three PNGs retain
their generated RGBA alpha, including the translucent light around each object.
Reference images were viewed from `../mosh-redesign` and described in the prompts.

- `welcome.png`: shield with person, chat and lock tiles; 1254 × 1254.
- `devices.png`: laptop and phone connected through a shield; 1774 × 887.
- `network.png`: shield with globe, Wi-Fi and laptop tiles; 1254 × 1254.

Each asset used this common prompt followed by its subject prompt:

```text

Premium restrained 3D translucent smoked-black glass, lime green light (#B7D84A base, #95ee45 luminous highlights), fine silver-white outlines, rounded corners, subtle depth. Thin elliptical orbital ribbons, small lime particles and delicate dotted arcs. Render on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background with preserved soft transparent glow, no dark background rectangle, no text, no labels, no letters, no watermark, no extra composition outside subject. Centered with generous transparent margins. Designed to look sharp and elegant on a #0B0C0D background. Use one consistent material and lighting style for a series of three illustrations.
```
