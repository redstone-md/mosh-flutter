Mosh is a messenger for private chats, groups and calls that works without a central message server. Devices find each other automatically through the Moss peer-to-peer network, and messages travel directly between them or through a relay when a direct path is blocked.

You start with a display name. Mosh does not ask for a phone number or an email address, and each installation creates its own keys.

Private chats and groups are end-to-end encrypted, so only their members can read them. Public channels are open to anyone who joins, and a signature confirms who wrote each message. Devices find each other through public trackers: Mosh protects what you say, but does not hide that you are online.

Mosh+ is an optional supporter badge shown next to your name. It does not unlock or limit any messaging or calling features.

Mosh is free software under the GNU GPL v3. The source code is on GitHub.
