# Contributing to Mosh

Bug reports and ideas go to
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/redstone-md/mosh-flutter/issues).
[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) lists the checks a change has to pass, and the
[architecture map](docs/Architecture.md) explains the runtime boundaries.

## Contributor License Agreement

The copyright holders publish Mosh in application stores, which needs rights
beyond the GPL-3.0 for every part of the code. To keep that possible, a pull
request from anyone other than ForeverInLaw or Rxflex can be merged only after
its author accepts the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). You keep the
copyright in your code.

To accept it, put this line in the pull request description:

```text
I have read CLA.md and agree to its terms for this and all my future contributions to Mosh.
```

Accepting it once covers all your later pull requests. If you contribute on
behalf of your employer, make sure you are allowed to grant the licenses in
the agreement.
